3. Engineering Heuristic Safety Audit

Deterministic safety rules monitor environmental thresholds to trigger immediate risk escalation regardless of raw statistical outputs:

  • Critical Storm Slab Rule: Triggered in 58.5% of locations ($\text{Slope} \ge 34^\circ \land \text{Snow}_{24\text{h}} \ge 30\text{mm}$).
  • Severe Wind Loading Rule: Triggered in 64.6% of locations ($\text{Wind} \ge 65\text{km/h} \land \text{Slope} \ge 36^\circ$).
